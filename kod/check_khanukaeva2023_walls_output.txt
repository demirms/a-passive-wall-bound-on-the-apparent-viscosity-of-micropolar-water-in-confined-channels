A  Constant map Nowacki form (Khanukaeva & Troshkin, p. 072005-4) -> Eringen form (our Eqs. (1)-(4))
====================================================================================================
  from the balance equations: mu = -j + mu_N , kappa = 2*j , gamma = delta + epsilon , alpha + beta = alpha_N + delta - epsilon
  spin-equation check 2 kappa - 4 j = 0
  couple-stress split (constitutive): beta = delta - eps, alpha = alpha_N; residual of alpha+beta: 0
  mu + kappa/2 = mu_N   (= mu_N: their mu is the Newtonian viscosity eta0)
  kappa/2 = j   (= j: their rotational viscosity is eta_r of Hansen 2010)
  N^2 = j/(mu_N+j) = kappa/[2(mu+kappa)] = eta_r/(eta0+eta_r);  check: 0
  (N/L)^2 - (lambda R)^2 = 0
  1/(2 N^2) - (1 + mu/kappa) = 0  (their bound on n = pole n_p of our BC2)
  1/(1 - N^2) - (mu+kappa)/(mu+kappa/2) = 0  (bound 1 + eta_r/eta0)
